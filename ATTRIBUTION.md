# Provenance and redistribution

Source recovered from Felipe Martínez's UPC-FIB SO2 Drive folder, `zeos23-4`, including its `include` directory. ZeOS is a course-provided teaching kernel with student modifications; the full kernel must not be presented as an independently authored operating system. A pristine matching starter tree and collaborator history were unavailable.

Original copyright and author comments are retained, including the Linus Torvalds, Martin Mares and Zeus Gómez notices in `build.c`. Some structures/helpers follow Linux teaching-kernel conventions. 

Migration additions are documentation and the host regression test. The three targeted repairs are listed in the README. Compiled libraries, objects, images, generated assembly, backups and unrelated notes were excluded.

The repository owner confirmed on 24 November 2026 that they hold the necessary redistribution rights for the coursework and included third-party material. Original author credits and copyright notices are retained; no new license is assigned to inherited code. The omitted `libzeos.a` or its source is still needed to complete the full build.
